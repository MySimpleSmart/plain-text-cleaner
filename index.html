How it works

  1. 01Paste or open a file. Add text from anywhere, or open a TXT, CSV, or TSV file.
  2. 02Clean automatically. Extra spaces, blank lines, and common markup are cleaned on paste. You can adjust the rules in Settings.
  3. 03Copy or export. Copy clean text with tables, download a TXT file, or print to PDF.

Your text is processed in your browser. Draft saving is off by default and can be enabled in Settings.
